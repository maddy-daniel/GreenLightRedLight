Privacy Policy - Green Light/Red Light

Last Updated: 7/13/26

Developer: Maddy Daniel

App Name: Green Light/Red Light

Package Name: com.mads.greenlightredlight

Platform: Android

Overview

Green Light/Red Light ("the app") is a personal finance tracking application developed as a senior project at Fairleigh Dickinson University. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data Collection

Green Light/Red Light does not collect any user data.

The app does not: 

    Collect personal information
    
    Require an account or login
    
    Connect to the internet
    
    Send data to any server
    
    Share data with third parties
    
    Use analytics or tracking tools
    
    Display advertisements

Data Storage

All data entered in the app(income entries, expense entries, and settings) is stored locally on your device only using Android's Room Database. This data never leaves your device and is only accessible through the app.

When you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is permanently deleted from your device.

Permissions

Green Light/Red Light requests the following permission: 
  
  Write External Storage - used only to save CSV files to your device's Downloads folder when you tap the Download as CSV button. No data is uploaded or shared anywhere.

Children's Privacy

Green Light/Red Light does not collect any data from anyone including children under the age of 13.
